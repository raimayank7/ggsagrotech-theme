Common questions

What is powder coating?

It is a way of finishing metal with a dry coloured powder instead of liquid paint. The powder is sprayed on and then baked, so it melts and hardens into one smooth, durable layer.

Is powder coating better than paint?

For most metal parts, yes. The baked coat is usually thicker and harder than paint, so it resists chipping, scratches and rust better and keeps its colour longer.
